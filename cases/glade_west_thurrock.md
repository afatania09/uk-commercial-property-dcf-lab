# Case 01 — The Glade Business Centre, West Thurrock

## Purpose
A public-information case study showing how a valuer could structure an explicit DCF investigation for a reversionary multi-let industrial estate. This is **not** a valuation of the property and the model must not be represented as one.

## Public facts recorded from the marketing page
Source: Allsop, *Prominent Reversionary Freehold Multi-let Industrial Estate*, The Glade Business Centre, Eastern Avenue, West Thurrock, RM20 3FH (accessed September 2026).

- Freehold multi-let industrial estate.
- 10 units.
- 17,684 sq ft ground-floor GIA plus 4,295 sq ft first-floor accommodation.
- Nine tenants.
- WAULT stated as 3.95 years to expiries and 2.54 years to breaks.
- Passing rent stated as £332,172 pa, equating to £18.78 psf excluding first-floor accommodation.
- Latest on-site letting stated at £22.47 psf.
- Marketing status: sold; offers sought in excess of £5,000,000; quoted NIY 6.23%.

Public source: https://www.allsop.co.uk/investment-overview/prominent-reversionary-freehold-multilet-industrial-estate-in-west-thurrock/ci00094

## What can legitimately be concluded from those facts?
The marketing evidence indicates a reversionary investment: the latest stated on-site letting is above the average passing rent. That is a **signal for investigation**, not proof that every unit has an ERV of £22.47 psf. Unit size, specification, lease date, incentives, frontage/access, first-floor accommodation and individual tenancy terms require analysis.

## DCF questions a valuer must answer
1. What is the appropriate ERV for each unit at the valuation date?
2. Which lease events are contractually expected during the explicit period?
3. How should breaks be treated? A break is an option, not automatically a vacancy event.
4. What market evidence supports rental growth?
5. What probability/allowance is appropriate for voids, rent-free periods, letting fees and capex?
6. What market evidence supports the discount rate?
7. What evidence supports the exit yield, and should it differ from the entry yield?
8. Is the exercise estimating **Market Value** using market-participant assumptions, or **Investment Value** using entity-specific assumptions?

## Illustrative model only
The repository engine can model annual net cash flows and a terminal value. A future version of this case will populate the cash flow only after each material assumption is tied to public evidence. Until then, no point estimate is presented: false precision would be worse valuation practice than leaving an assumption unresolved.

## Cross-checks
A completed case should reconcile and explain:
- quoted NIY / transaction evidence;
- an implicit income-capitalisation cross-check;
- explicit DCF indication;
- sensitivity to discount rate, exit yield and rental growth;
- any difference between model indication and observed transaction price.

## Professional judgement
The code is the easy part. The valuation task is selecting, verifying and weighting evidence and explaining why the assumptions are appropriate. The model therefore exposes assumptions rather than hiding them.
